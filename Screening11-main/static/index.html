    What happens

    1. Read documents. Text from the deck and your extra files.
    2. Crawl the website. Sitemap and links, ranked by relevance; robots.txt respected; broken links reported.
    3. Extract claims. Each claim keeps an exact quote, which is checked against the source.
    4. Search the web. Funding, team, traction and adverse news; sources classed as independent, self-reported or company-owned.
    5. Verify. A claim is only "verified" if an independent source confirms it.
    6. Score. Weights adapt to stage and business model; contradictions lower the score; every adjustment is explained.

    Your documents and website text are sent to your AI provider for analysis and search queries go to a public search engine. Results are stored only where this app runs.

      Screen without a fund thesis?

      A fund thesis tells the app what you invest in, so it can check the company against your countries, stages, sectors and round size and weight the scores your way. Results are better with one.

      Without it this will be an open screening: any country, stage and sector, standard thresholds (advance at 7, maybe at 5.5) and the default weight for each scoring dimension.
